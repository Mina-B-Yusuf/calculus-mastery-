# Calculus 1 & 2 Study Guide

Built from *Calculus: A Complete Course*, 10th Edition, by Robert A. Adams & Christopher Essex (the book PDFs live in [`../Calculus - A Complete Course, 10th Edition/`](<../Calculus - A Complete Course, 10th Edition/>)). Every chapter below reproduces the book's definitions, theorems, and formulas in clean notation, adds fully worked examples and "common mistakes" callouts, and ends with a 15-problem review set with complete solutions.

Also see [`formula-sheet.md`](formula-sheet.md) — a single-page-style quick reference covering both courses, good for last-minute review the night before the exam.

## How to use this

1. **First pass**: read each chapter's definitions/theorems/formulas and worked examples in order — don't skip Chapter P even if it feels basic, algebra slips are the #1 cause of lost exam points.
2. **Second pass**: close the notes and do every chapter's Review Problems cold, then check against the Solutions section. Redo anything you got wrong the next day.
3. **Week of the exam**: use `formula-sheet.md` for rapid-fire recall, and redo the Review Problems for your weakest 2–3 chapters again.
4. If you have actual past exam papers from your professor, add them to this repo (e.g. a `past-papers/` folder) and mention it — the notes can be extended with problems in that exact style.
